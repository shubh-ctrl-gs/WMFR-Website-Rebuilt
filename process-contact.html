OCCUPIED RENOVATION METHODOLOGY

How We Turn 3 Units Per Trade Per Day — While Occupied

Most contractors struggle when residents are living steps away. WMFR's proprietary trade rotation system eliminates downtime, noise friction, and costly schedule slip.

01

Tenant Concierge & 72-Hour Staging

Dedicated on-site tenant liaison teams communicate with residents 72 hours in advance, providing clear daily schedules, high-grade floor & furniture protective coverings, and zero-surprise site access.

02

Self-Performing Master Trades

We never broker out critical path work. Our full-time, in-house master trades handle demolition, MEP, luxury quartz countertops, bespoke cabinetry, and acoustic flooring with consistent quality.

03

Same-Unit Daily Turnover Sprints

Each unit is managed in strict 8-hour sprint blocks. Trades enter in choreographed waves, completing inspections same-day to keep the project on rapid institutional pace.

04

Zero-Punch Clean Daily Handoff

Rigorous white-glove QA inspections at 4:30 PM daily ensure each unit is clean, secure, and ready for immediate resident enjoyment or leasing turnover without open punch-lists.
