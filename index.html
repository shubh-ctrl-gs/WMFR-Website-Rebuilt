OCCUPIED RENOVATION METHODOLOGY

How We Turn 3 Units Per Trade Per Day — While Occupied

Most contractors struggle when residents are living steps away. WMFR's proprietary trade rotation system eliminates downtime, noise complaints, and costly schedule slip.

01

Tenant Concierge & Staging

Dedicated on-site tenant liaison teams communicate with residents 72 hours in advance, providing clear schedules, protective coverings, and zero-surprise access.

02

Self-Performing Crews

We do not broker out critical path work. Our full-time, in-house master trades handle demolition, MEP, quartz countertops, custom cabinetry, and tile installations.

03

Same-Unit Daily Turnover

Each unit is managed in strict 8-hour sprint blocks. Trades enter in choreographed waves, completing inspections same-day to keep the project on rapid pace.

04

Zero-Punch Clean Handoff

Rigorous white-glove QA inspections at 4:30 PM daily ensure each unit is clean, secure, and ready for immediate residential enjoyment or leasing turnover.
